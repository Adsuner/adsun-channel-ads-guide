# Public channel advertising setup

1. Add your public channel as a platform in the Adsun publisher panel.
2. Make @AdsunChannelAdvBot an administrator following the permission instructions in the official channel-platform guide; do not give unrelated permissions.
3. Wait for moderation approval.
4. Create a Channel block and select up to 3 daily posts and posting slots.
5. The managed bot publishes sponsored posts with advertiser link, image/text, advertising mark and button.
6. Posts expire after 24 hours. If removal fails, delete manually; views after 24 hours are not paid.

Official guides: https://adsun.io/documentation/publisher/channel-platform, https://adsun.io/documentation/publisher/channel-moderation, https://adsun.io/documentation/publisher/channel-block, https://adsun.io/documentation/publisher/channel-payments.

No channel request endpoint is documented in this guide. This repository does not automate posting or claim approval.
